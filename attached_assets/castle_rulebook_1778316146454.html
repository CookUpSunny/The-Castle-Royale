The Game
What is Castle?
A card-shedding game of nerve, strategy, and a little luck
1
The Objective
Be the first player to rid yourself of every card — your hand first, then your face-up castle cards, then your blind face-down cards.
2
The Shared Pile
A central discard pile sits at the table. On your turn, you must play a card equal to or higher in rank than the top card of the pile.
3
Cannot Play?
If you hold no valid card to play, you must collect the entire pile into your hand — and you may also leave your lowest card if you so choose.
4
The Loser
The last player still holding cards when all others have finished loses the round — and earns the title.

Gameplay
How a Turn Works
Play → draw → pass — repeat until someone wins
1
Play a Card — or a Pair, Three of a Kind, or Four of a Kind
Play one or more cards of the same rank from your hand. You may play a single card, two of a kind, three of a kind, or all four of a kind at once. All cards must be equal to or higher in rank than the top card of the pile.
2
Refill to Three
After playing, draw from the deck until you hold three cards in hand again — as long as the deck has cards remaining.
3
Cannot Play? Collect the Pile
If no card in your hand is valid to play, collect every card from the pile into your hand. You may also leave your lowest card if you so choose.
4
Hand Empty & Deck Gone — Play Face-Ups
Once your hand is empty and the deck is exhausted, begin playing your face-up cards. Same rules — equal or higher to play.
5
Face-Ups Gone — Flip Blind
Choose any face-down card without looking at it. Flip it. Plays legally? Go. Doesn't? Collect the pile and that card into your hand.

10
The Burn
Destroys the entire pile — all cards permanently removed from play. The player who burns takes another turn immediately. Devastating in the late game.
Bonus burn: Playing four cards of the same rank at once also burns the pile — same effect as a 10. You play again immediately.
The House Rules
Rules That Matter
Six rulings every player must know before cards are dealt
Legal Equal Rank
Playing the same rank as the top card is always a valid play. Equal counts.
Legal Multi-Play
You may play two, three, or four cards of the same rank in a single turn. All count as one play.
Illegal Skip Zones
You may not play face-ups while hand cards remain. You may not play face-downs while face-ups remain.
Trap Blind Flip Fails
If your flipped face-down card cannot be played, you collect the pile and that card into your hand. You must then play a card.
Rule Ace is Highest
Ace beats everything in the game — except a 2 wild or a 10 burn. Those two cards override the Ace.
Rule Drawing from Deck
You cannot draw from the deck if you already hold more than 3 cards in your hand. Get back down to 3 cards before you draw again.
