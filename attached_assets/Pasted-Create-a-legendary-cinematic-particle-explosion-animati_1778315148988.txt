Create a legendary cinematic particle explosion animation for a luxury multiplayer mobile card game called “Castle Royale.”

The explosion is triggered when a player successfully plays a “10 four-of-a-kind” combo. This is one of the rarest and most powerful moments in the game, so the visual effect must feel mythic, explosive, luxurious, and unforgettable.

STYLE:

* AAA game-quality motion graphics
* Premium casino mythology aesthetic
* Unreal Engine / high-end mobile VFX feel
* Smooth 60fps
* Hyper-polished cinematic animation
* Not cartoony
* Volumetric and layered with realistic depth

SCENE:
The four “10” cards slam together in the center of the arena table.
There is a brief anticipation pause before detonation.
The environment darkens slightly.
A glowing magical golden rune appears beneath the cards.

Then:
A massive explosion erupts outward from the center.

PARTICLE SYSTEM:
Use hundreds or thousands of independently simulated particles.

Every particle should have:

* randomized velocity
* randomized scale
* unique direction
* rotation
* motion blur
* fade timing
* drag/friction
* lifespan decay
* turbulence influence

PARTICLE TYPES:

* golden sparks
* glowing embers
* smoke wisps
* magical rune fragments
* floating ash
* card fragments
* neon energy shards
* plasma trails
* glowing dust
* lightning arcs
* energy streaks

MOTION:

* explosive radial burst
* particles spiral naturally
* layered turbulence fields
* secondary micro explosions
* subtle vortex motion near the blast center
* some particles travel extremely fast
* others drift cinematically in slow motion

LIGHTING:

* white-hot center flash
* gold/orange/red/emerald energy tones
* dynamic bloom
* volumetric lighting
* cinematic glow
* light scattering through smoke
* reflective highlights on nearby cards
* pulsing illumination from explosion core

SMOKE & ENERGY:

* dense cinematic smoke cloud
* smoke curls realistically
* energy trails stretch behind particles
* subtle heat distortion/refraction
* magical energy waves ripple through the environment

CAMERA FX:

* cinematic camera shake
* subtle zoom-in during impact
* motion blur during detonation
* temporary exposure bloom
* dramatic depth-of-field shift

ENVIRONMENT REACTION:

* nearby cards react to shockwave
* loose cards lift from table slightly
* arena lights pulse from the explosion
* glossy surfaces reflect the blast
* UI elements glow dynamically from emitted light

COLOR PALETTE:

* gold
* white-hot energy
* crimson red
* emerald green
* electric orange
* black smoke
* subtle cosmic purple accents

TIMING:

* anticipation pause: 0.15 seconds
* main detonation: 0.4 seconds
* debris/smoke linger: 2 seconds

OVERALL FEELING:
The player should instantly feel:
“Legendary move activated.”
“The pile has been obliterated.”
“Casino god power.”
“Rare combo achieved.”

The effect should feel addictive, elite, rewarding, cinematic, and worthy of replay moments in a premium competitive mobile game.
