Castle — SFX audition

Open this file in Chrome or Safari (double-click, or “Open with…”). Regenerate all cues with node scripts/generate-sfx.js from artifacts/mobile.

In-game wiring: match start → fanfare-epic.wav (fanfare-epic). Playing a 2 or 10 → slot-alt-b-bright.wav (slot-bright). First castle (hand cleared, face-up row remains) → fanfare-opt-e-cathedral.wav (fanfare-cathedral). Bottom castle (face-up cleared, face-down remains) → fanfare-epic.wav (fanfare-epic). Full match win → fanfare-victory.wav (fanfare-victory): long **slot-machine-style jackpot** — spin ticks, three reel locks, fast credit bleeps, hopper pour, then major chord + sparkles (~4.4s incl. ring-out). Swap sounds by overwriting the bundled WAV or changing require() in lib/sfx.ts.

Match win — slot jackpot
